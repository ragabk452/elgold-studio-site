## Project Description

Gold Skincare is a premium e-commerce store concept for a beauty and skincare brand. The design direction focuses on trust, clarity, product discovery, and a clean shopping experience that can scale into a full store.

The project covers the customer-facing experience and the business logic behind the store: categories, cart, checkout, order tracking, and product/order management direction.

## Key Features

- Premium beauty e-commerce visual direction.
- Product categories and clean browsing.
- Cart and checkout user flow.
- Order tracking direction.
- Responsive store experience.
- Business-friendly product/order management thinking.
